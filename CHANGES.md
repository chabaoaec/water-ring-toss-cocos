- Short taps produce weaker/slower flow; holding ramps speed and force until the budget runs out.

- Rings rebound elastically against each other; shell/post response is softer and water dissipates motion.
- Scoring requires actual platform-rooted support and conservative peg containment; hovering rings no longer qualify.
